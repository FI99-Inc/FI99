FI99 is short for Future Idiots.

You would have found out eventually. We would rather you heard it from us.

In September 2019 the three of us made a group chat and gave it a stupid name,
the way you do when the name is never going to leave the chat. It left the chat.
The group chat still exists (across almost every social media platform now), and
almost seven years of ideas, dead projects, and arguments sits under a banner
that says Future Idiots.

When we went to put that on something real, we heard it properly for the first
time. Future Idiots is a great name for three friends. But that is about it. The
99 does not mean anything. We liked how it looked next to the FI. If you were
hoping for better, we do not have better, and we are not going to invent it for
a story.

## Oakville

We are from Oakville, and, ironically, we arrived and left in the same order.

Lars got there first, before kindergarten, and left first, the summer before
Grade 7. Srihith arrived in Grade 2 and left second, after Grade 12, when his
family moved to Ajax. Omar arrived last, in Grade 5, and has not left (yet).

The chat is dated September 2019. Lars had moved that summer. We are not going
to pretend we knew what we were doing. But that is when the friendship stopped
being something the town arranged for us and became something we kept up on
purpose.

Then COVID turned the chat into the entire friendship. Middle school went
online. We played a lot of Xbox. For two years the most reliable thing any of us
had was a group chat with a stupid name, and it held.

## What it is for

Nothing was wrong. There is no villain here. No bad internship, no boss, nobody
telling us to wait our turn. What happened is simpler, and harder to say without
sounding pleased with ourselves: we looked at the people we had spent years
idolizing and worked out that we had the same three ingredients they did. The
potential. The ambition. The dedication to keep going after the interesting part
is over. FI99 is us finding out whether that is true.

We had talked about this for years and never seriously. Then we finished our
first year of university a few months ago, found a summer in front of us, and
did it. That is the entire origin story. Some things do not need a moment.

The distance helped more than it hurt. Srihith and Omar are in Toronto, Lars is
in Sudbury, and you see an idea more clearly when you have to explain it to
someone who is not in the room.

So FI99 is a place to learn where it can be checked. We are not picking a
category. We would rather be the studio that could plausibly ship anything.
Maybe that changes, maybe it does not.

Some of what we make will be useful. Some of it will be a joke that got out of
hand. All of it ships, because, in our eyes, a thing that does not ship did not
happen.

We are not precious about where it goes, either. If you want something built, we
will build it. If one of these outgrows the studio, we will follow it. If
someone wants to buy a product, or the studio, that is a conversation we are
willing to have.

We are still the same group of childhood friends from Glen Abbey. We just have a
domain now. And the means to see it through.

Want something built, or want to argue about whether it should exist? Perhaps
you just want to talk to Lars about LEGO or Omar about how Barcelona is the best
soccer (or football) team. Either way you know what to do.
